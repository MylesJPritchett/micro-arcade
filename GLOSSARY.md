# Micro Arcade

A personal collection of small browser arcade games, built on crisp-game-lib and played from one site.

## Language

**Arcade**:
The whole collection of Games and the site that hosts them, named "Micro Arcade".
_Avoid_: Game Collection, crisp-game-lib (that's the engine, not the product)

**Game**:
One self-contained playable title in the Arcade, such as Dodge or Duel.
_Avoid_: App, level

**Dashboard**:
The Arcade's landing page that lists every Game.
_Avoid_: Home, index, menu

**Game Card**:
One tile on the Dashboard representing a single Game, showing its preview and High Score.
_Avoid_: Tile, thumbnail

**High Score**:
The best score a player has reached on a Game on their own device.
_Avoid_: Best, record
